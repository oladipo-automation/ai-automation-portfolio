# ai-automation-portfolio
Welcome to my AI Automation Portfolio.  
I am learning AI automation by building real projects using Make.com, Zapier, Google Sheets, AI text tools, and no-code automation platforms.

📂 Projects Included
🔹 1. Make – Automated Content Formatter + Email Sender
What it does:
Takes raw text from Google Sheets → uses Make AI & Text Aggregator → generates a clean structured summary and send to an email.

Skills demonstrated:
- Triggers (Google Sheets Watch Rows)  
- Text Aggregator formatting  
- Gmail automation

🔹 2. Zapier – Job Application Tracker
What it does:
Every time I add a job to Google Sheets (company, role, follow-up date), Zapier automatically schedules a reminder and sends me an email on that date.

Skills demonstrated:
- Zapier New Row Trigger  
- Delay Until  
- Zapoer AI
- Automated email reminders
